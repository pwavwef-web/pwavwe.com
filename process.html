PROCESS

Fewer surprises.
Better decisions.

A clear process protects the idea, the budget and the people doing the work. Every phase has a purpose.

  1. 01

    Request

    You describe the idea, problem, intended users, timeline and available budget.

  2. 02

    Clarify

    Francis reviews the request and asks the questions needed to understand the work.

  3. 03

    Scope

    The project becomes agreed features, milestones, responsibilities and exclusions.

  4. 04

    Propose

    You receive a proposal covering scope, schedule and pricing.

  5. 05

    Design

    The product structure, interface and user journey are designed.

  6. 06

    Build

    The agreed product is developed in useful, reviewable milestones.

  7. 07

    Test

    Relevant devices, browsers and user journeys are checked before launch.

  8. 08

    Launch

    The product is deployed, documented and handed over.

  9. 09

    Support

    Agreed maintenance, monitoring or future improvements continue after launch.

Good to know: Submitting a request does not guarantee acceptance, create a contract or commit you to payment. Scope, schedule and pricing exist only after they are agreed in writing.
