01 / The fit

A practical starting point for people with something to solve.

Designed for Ghanaian learners with curiosity, care and a real problem to make progress on. No coding experience is required.

This cohort is for

  • Ghanaian final-year university students
  • Recent graduates
  • National-service personnel
  • Selected early-career professionals
  • Curious non-technical learners with a real problem to solve

Probably not the right fit: experienced machine-learning engineers or people interested only in obtaining a certificate.

04 / What you receive

The tools, practice and feedback to keep going.

The aim is useful work you can explain and improve, not a week of passive watching.

  • Three live practical workshops
  • Four guided learning and project activities
  • A digital workbook and reusable templates
  • One completed AI-assisted project
  • A documented human–AI workflow
  • Project testing and rubric-based feedback
  • A reusable personal prompt library
  • A portfolio-ready project case study
  • Recordings available for 30 days
  • Access to the learner community for 30 days
  • Certificate of Completion for qualifying learners
  • Eligibility to apply for the four-week Pwavwe AI Builder Lab

05 / The final project

Build something genuinely useful.

Use AI to address a real problem in a context you understand. A logo or a few generated social posts alone will not qualify.

Choose a direction

  • Study and research
  • Career or professional work
  • Business or entrepreneurship
  • Content and creativity
  • Tourism or hospitality
  • Community or social impact
  • Personal productivity

Show how you got there

  1. The problem and intended user
  2. Prompts and iterations
  3. Human decisions and revisions
  4. Verification and responsible use
  5. A completed output
  6. One peer or user test
  7. A short final demonstration

06 / Completion

Earn a Certificate of Completion.

This recognises completed learning and a quality project. It is not an “AI expert” or engineering qualification.

To qualify, you must

  • Complete at least six of the seven required activities
  • Submit the final project
  • Complete the baseline and final assessments
  • Meet the project-quality threshold
  • Avoid plagiarism, fabrication and serious privacy violations

Attending sessions alone does not guarantee a certificate.

07 / Optional bonus

Explore Google Cloud with guidance.

Eligible new users will receive guided support to check their eligibility for Google Cloud’s $300 welcome credit and safely configure Firebase or supported Google Cloud services.

Eligibility is determined by Google. A valid payment method may be required for verification. The credit is not issued or guaranteed by Pwavwe AI Academy and is not required to complete the programme.

09 / Questions

Good questions, clear answers.

How the founding cohort worked.

Is the programme really free?

Yes. The seven-day founding cohort is free. The optional Google Cloud offer is separate and is not required.

Do I need coding experience?

No. The programme is designed for beginners and practical, non-technical learners.

Does joining WhatsApp guarantee admission?

No. You must complete the application form to be considered. Joining the community alone does not qualify you for training.

How are applicants selected?

Applications are reviewed for audience fit, motivation, a useful problem to work on and the ability to take part. There are 12–15 founding places.

What must I do to receive the certificate?

Complete at least six of seven required activities, submit a quality final project, complete both assessments and meet the integrity requirements above.

What kind of project can I build?

Choose a real problem in study, work, business, creativity, tourism, community impact or personal productivity. Show your process, a completed output and one peer or user test.

Is the Google Cloud credit guaranteed?

No. Google determines eligibility, may require a valid payment method for verification and issues any credit directly. You do not need it for the programme.

Can I participate using only a smartphone?

Yes, although a laptop can make research, documentation and project work easier. You will need reliable internet access for live sessions and activities.

What happens after the seven-day programme?

You can use your project and prompt library independently. Qualifying learners may apply for the four-week Pwavwe AI Builder Lab; participation in Foundations does not guarantee a place.

Will the live sessions be recorded?

Yes. Recordings will be available to learners for 30 days.
