Mobile application · Student app, 2025–2026

AZ Learner

A student app that put UCC academic life in one place: an AI study assistant, timetable, notes, GPA tools and campus services.

PROJECT NOTES

Status
Student app, 2025–2026
Users
University of Cape Coast students.
Technology
Flutter · Firebase Auth, Firestore, Storage and Messaging · Gemini via Firebase AI Logic
01

The original problem

Students juggle timetables, notes, GPA planning, event tickets and campus errands across scattered apps, chats and noticeboards.

02

Who experienced it

University of Cape Coast students.

03

What was built

A cross-platform Flutter app with a Gemini-powered study assistant, timetable, tasks and notes, a GPA calculator, event tickets, direct chat and print-and-deliver requests.

  • AZ AI study assistant
  • Timetable, tasks and notes
  • GPA calculator
  • Event tickets with Paystack
  • Direct chat and campus services
04

Technical approach

Flutter, Firebase Auth, Firestore, Storage and Messaging, Gemini via Firebase AI Logic. The implementation follows the needs of this product; the technology list is not treated as the outcome.

05

Difficult decisions

A student super-app has to stay useful as needs change without accumulating features that obscure the core study journey.

06

Result or current status

Student app, 2025–2026. The app’s original website, azlearner.me, now hosts AZ Studio, Francis’s separate creative workspace for films and audio. This page is now the home that explains the app.

07

Lessons

Founder-led products improve through repeated contact with the actual problem, not a one-time feature list.
