PROJECT NOTES

Status
Live platform
Users
Voters, election officers and the organisations that run elections, starting with student associations at the University of Cape Coast.
Technology
React and TypeScript · Firebase-backed workflows · Role-aware administration
01

The original problem

Elections need a controlled voting journey, clear administration and results that can be checked without making the experience confusing for voters.

02

Who experienced it

Voters, election officers and the organisations that run elections, starting with student associations at the University of Cape Coast.

03

What was built

A managed elections platform covering voter setup and voter-code access, configurable ballots, staff and polling-agent dashboards, audit trails, certified results and election-scoped exports.

  • Voter setup and voter-code access
  • Configurable ballots
  • Staff and polling-agent dashboards
  • Audit trails
  • Certified public results and exports
04

Technical approach

React and TypeScript, Firebase-backed workflows, Role-aware administration. The implementation follows the needs of this product; the technology list is not treated as the outcome.

05

Difficult decisions

Election software must balance ease of participation with deliberate controls, traceability and clear operational boundaries.

06

Result or current status

Live platform. This case study intentionally avoids claims that cannot be verified from the project record.

07

Lessons

Trust comes from understandable rules, careful state changes and evidence that administrators can inspect after the vote.
